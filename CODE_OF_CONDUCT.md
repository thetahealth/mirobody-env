# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person.

Unacceptable: harassment, personal attacks, publishing others' private information,
and sustained disruption of discussion.

## One rule specific to this project

This is a benchmark repository for medical AI evaluation. Two things follow:

1. **All patient data here is synthetic and is not medical advice.** Do not present numbers
   from this repository as clinical evidence, and do not open issues asking for clinical
   guidance.
2. **Claims are expected to be checkable.** Disagreement about a number is normal and welcome;
   the way to settle it is to point at a file, a command, or a reading — not at seniority. A
   reviewer who finds an error in this repository's own output is doing the project a service,
   and that includes errors made by maintainers.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to the repository maintainers via a private channel (repository
security advisory or the contact listed in the repository metadata). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow this document are subject to it equally.
